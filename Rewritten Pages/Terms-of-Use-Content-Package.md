## What the website provides

Grams to Cups offers ingredient-based measurement conversions and practical measuring information for cooking and baking. The calculators and reference pages are available without an account.

The website provides general information. It does not test your ingredients, inspect your measuring equipment or verify the instructions in a recipe you are following.

## Understanding conversion results

Our results are estimates calculated from selected ingredient references and cup volumes. The amount you measure can differ because of the product, preparation, packing, measuring method or equipment you use. Rounded decimal answers and practical cup-and-spoon suggestions are not promises of an exact physical measurement.

Before using a result, check the ingredient, whether it is raw or prepared, and the cup standard. Follow a recipe's own gram measurements or ingredient-specific instructions when provided. If you have a scale and a stated gram amount, you can weigh that amount directly.

The [methodology](https://thegramstocups.com/methodology/), [grams-per-cup chart](https://thegramstocups.com/grams-per-cup/) and [cup-size guide](https://thegramstocups.com/cup-sizes/) explain the references and assumptions. A numerical conversion does not establish that one ingredient can replace another in a recipe.

These culinary tools are not designed for medication dosing, laboratory measurements or other uses requiring a validated measurement system. They do not provide nutritional, medical or food-safety assessments.
