Understanding conversion results

Our results are estimates calculated from selected ingredient references and cup volumes. The amount you measure can differ because of the product, preparation, packing, measuring method or equipment you use. Rounded decimal answers and practical cup-and-spoon suggestions are not promises of an exact physical measurement.

Before using a result, check the ingredient, whether it is raw or prepared, and the cup standard. Follow a recipe's own gram measurements or ingredient-specific instructions when provided. If you have a scale and a stated gram amount, you can weigh that amount directly.
