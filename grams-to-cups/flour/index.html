Why Flour Weight Varies: Spooning vs Scooping

If you dip a measuring cup directly into a bag of flour, you scoop compressed flour. A scooped cup can weigh anywhere from 135g to 140g. Using a scooped cup in a recipe developed with a 120g spooned cup adds up to 20% extra flour, resulting in dense, dry bread or rubbery cookies.

How to Spoon & Level Flour: Fluff your flour with a fork, gently spoon it into the measuring cup until overflowing, and sweep off the excess with the flat back of a knife.
